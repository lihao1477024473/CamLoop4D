CamLoop4D: Supplementary Material

CamLoop4D: Controllable 4D Scene Generation by Closing the Camera Loop between Generation and Reconstruction

Supplementary Material

H. Li1, J. Chen2 · 1Independent Researcher · 2Tsinghua University · Pacific Graphics 2026 (CGF Journal Track)

CamLoop4D couples a camera-conditioned video diffusion model with a 4D Gaussian reconstructor through one shared Plücker trajectory tensor, removing the fragile pose re-estimation step of cascaded pipelines. Each case below shows the camera-controlled generated video (left) alongside the reconstructed 4D Gaussian rendering (right) produced under the same shared trajectory, on a single RTX 3090.

Quantitative Results (Per-Scene Metrics)

The unrounded per-scene numbers behind the appendix reconstruction tables are shown directly below (single run, fixed seed 0). Per-scene deltas are heterogeneous and change sign — CamLoop4D loses to MoSca on the articulated block/hand/mochi-high-five scenes — consistent with the honest, modest aggregate gain reported in the appendix.
